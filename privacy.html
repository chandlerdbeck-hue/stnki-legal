stnki Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

stnki ("the app") is made by Chandler Beck ("we", "us"). Questions? Email chandler.d.beck@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your rankings and notes live on your phone.
  • We use your location only while the app is open, to show bathrooms near you.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

What the app uses

Your rankings, notes and bookmarks. Stored on your device. Deleting the app or using Settings > "Delete all data on this device" removes them.

Your location. If you allow it, the app uses your approximate or precise location while it is open to search for nearby bathrooms. Location is sent to Apple Maps to run that search, under Apple's privacy policy. We don't store your location history. You can turn location off any time in iOS Settings > stnki > Location.

Places you add. If you drop a pin to add a bathroom, that pin is saved with your other data.

Accounts and friends (when they launch)

When sign-in arrives (Sign in with Apple), we will store your name or handle, your rankings you choose to share, and who you follow, on our servers (hosted with a backend provider we will name here when accounts launch). Shared rankings are visible to friends according to your privacy settings. You will be able to delete your account and its data from Settings.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your data. Apple provides the maps and location search. Once accounts exist, our backend provider processes data for us only to run the app.

Children

stnki is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

You can change location permission in iOS Settings, delete local data in the app, and (once accounts exist) delete your account in Settings. You can also email us to ask about your data.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
